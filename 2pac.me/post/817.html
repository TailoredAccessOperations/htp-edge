Me Against the World

Published on July 24th, 2011

In his third studio album release, https://web.archive.org/web/20110815131211/http://www.2pac.com/Tupac Shakur’s “Me Against the World” debuted at Number 1 on the Billboard 200 charts, https://web.archive.org/web/20110815131211/http://www.2pac.com/surpassing the likes of Bruce Springstein’s Greatest Hits. The album certified double platinum before the end of the year and to date has sold nearly 2, https://web.archive.org/web/20110815131211/http://www.2pac.com/500, https://web.archive.org/web/20110815131211/http://www.2pac.com/000 copies. It was praised by the music industry with multiple Grammy Award nominations including Best Rap Album and Best Solo Rap Performance for the song ‘Dear Mama’. Dear Mama which become the album’s most successful single reached Number 1 on the Hot Rap Singles chart. Further accolades include About.com ranking “Me Against the World” as the 12th Greatest Hip-Hop Albums of All Time (2010). Below is a sampling of the album title song “Me Against the World”.

 

With all this extra stressing

The question I wonder is after death, after my last breath

When will I finally get to rest? Through this suppression

They punish the people that’s asking questions

 

And those that possess, steal from the ones without possessions

The message I stress to make it stop study your lessons

Don’t settle for less even the genius asks questions

Be grateful for blessings, don’t ever change, keep your essence

 

The power is in the people and politics we address

Always do your best, don’t let the pressure make you panic

And when you get stranded and things don’t go the way you planned it

Dreaming of riches, in a position of making a difference

 

Politicians and hypocrites, they don’t wanna listen

If I’m insane, it’s the fame made a brother change

It wasn’t nothing like the game

It’s just me against the world .
