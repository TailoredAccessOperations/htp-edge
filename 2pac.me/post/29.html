Antoine Fuqua takes on the 2Pac Story

Published on February 1st, 2011

“Nothin’ But Love”

Antoine Fuqua, director of the award-winning Training Day, https://web.archive.org/web/20120401022507/http://www.2pac.com/has agreed to direct an upcoming Tupac Shakur bio-picture with Morgan Creek Productions. Fans have waited a long time for this, https://web.archive.org/web/20120401022507/http://www.2pac.com/and Antoine is a veteran film-maker who will no-doubt do the Shakur story justice.

The film is currently casting for the roles of Afeni Shakur and Suge Knight among others. While there is no news on who will play the starring role, https://web.archive.org/web/20120401022507/http://www.2pac.com/we can expect to see a lot of new faces on screen. Antoine did say in a recent interview:

“I want to discover a lot of new people if I can. Obviously I’m going to have to put some people in it that you know, https://web.archive.org/web/20120401022507/http://www.2pac.com/just because actors have different skills. I want to go to the streets and find him anywhere he might be in the world.”

As more details about the new film open up, https://web.archive.org/web/20120401022507/http://www.2pac.com/you can expect 2Pac.com to get the dirt first. Stay tuned.
