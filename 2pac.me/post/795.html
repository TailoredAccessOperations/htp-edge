2Pac finally on iTunes

Published on July 5th, 2011

Hey hey everyone,

Today, five 2Pac albums (along with some singles) including the first three solo albums, will be issued to digital for the first time!

Finally, we can update our collections with 2Pacalypse Now, Strictly For My N.I.G.G.A.Z., Thug Life – Volume 1, Me Against The World, and R U Still Down? from iTunes.
