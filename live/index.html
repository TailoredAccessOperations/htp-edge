No lyric sheet.

Lyric sheets are not printed on this floor. ffmpeg can demux and decode a recording; it cannot recover a lyric, and this room does not transcribe commercial masters.

Literature

A compressed score.

Each album is read as a form: who is addressed, what the refrain is for, where the persona changes, how the picture is framed. Steps move through that argument. They are not timed to the master.

Impact and volume

Keep the voice in front.

Set the YouTube player near the middle of its own slider. If the kick covers the voice, lower the system bass instead of pushing the player to the top. Laptop speakers fold the low end, so a vocal-forward balance carries further than a boosted sub. On headphones, stop before the refrain saturates.

The grid under each score is drawn for this page. It is not a loudness meter and not a tempo measured from the recording.
