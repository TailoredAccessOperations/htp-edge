Nominatim queries are proxied (rate-limited) so your IP never touches third parties from the browser. The "model lead" is an inference, not ground truth — a competent analyst beats a confident zero-shot guess. Verify. Always verify.

    How the tool reads the frame

    • Extract — EXIF GPS, camera make/model, capture time and image bytes are read locally in the browser before anything leaves the machine.
    • Analyze — pixels are sent to the worker’s vision model, which returns a geolocation lead (lat/lon/radius/confidence) plus a chain of visual evidence: signage, script, architecture, road markings, vegetation, utility poles, geography.
    • Corroborate — the lead is pinned on OSM next to your EXIF fix, if any. Disagreeing sources mean a bigger radius, not a smaller ego.
    • Exploit — reverse-geocode the pin, jump a search to any place on Earth, flip to satellite, and copy coordinates out for further OSINT tooling.
